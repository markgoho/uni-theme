# Option D — “Patrol Poster” (key `D`, file `option-d.html`)

**Thesis:** energy for Scouts, not for institutions. Bold, flat, printed-poster graphics; big type; colour blocks; stickers and stamps from patrol culture (patches, flags, campfire). Loud but structured.

**Look:** flat colour blocks in a campfire palette: deep Scouting green, canvas tan, **brick red** and **marigold** — no gradients, no shadows, thick 2–3px rules and borders, generous radii on a few sticker-like pills only. Category/section colour coding (each MBU category gets a hue family; SU ranks progress through a hue ramp). Dark theme = chalkboard black-green ground with the same flat inks. Type: a loud display face (e.g. **Bricolage Grotesque**, **Archivo Black** or **Anton**) at very large sizes, paired with a friendly readable sans (e.g. **Nunito Sans** or **Work Sans**) for body. Uppercase display headings with tight tracking.

**Structural axes**
- Nav model: **top bar + big section tabs**: on award/requirements/guide, a full-width coloured tab strip (Overview · Requirements · Guide) directly under the header; no sidebar. Mobile: the same strip scrolls horizontally.
- Modes: **stamps**: a rotated sticker/stamp (“DO ALL” / “PICK ONE”) at the top corner of the requirement block, plus the choice children drawn as big lettered tiles.
- Requirement list form: **colour-blocked poster panels**: each top-level requirement is a full-width block with a giant number (display face, 4–6rem) and a coloured header band; children as chunky lettered rows inside; verbatim text in body sans at full size on a light panel; curated title as the big caps header. Resources as a small “more” row.
- Guide chrome: **zine spread**: a big coloured title block with the requirement quoted inside a bordered box, then wide body prose; callouts are full-bleed coloured bands with a stamp label (TIP / SAFETY FIRST / BE PREPARED / CHECKLIST); the guide nav is a horizontal chapter strip at the top, not a sidebar; prev/next as two giant arrow blocks.
- Search grouping: **tiles**: each award a colour-blocked tile with emblem (or big level number on SU) and its hits as stacked rows; query echoed in giant type at the top; count as a stamp.

**Home:** MBU — a poster: giant headline, hero photo cropped into a bold shape with a flat colour frame, categories as a wall of coloured tiles with counts, Eagle-required badges as a horizontally scrolling rail of patch cards with scroll-snap. SU — the trail to Eagle as **seven coloured stripes/blocks**, each rank a bold block with level number, name and 3 sample requirements; the two phases as big colour regions.

**Award page:** a poster for the award: giant name, emblem (MBU) or giant level numeral (SU) in a coloured block, facts as stickers, “what you’ll do” as a numbered list in big type, resources as chunky buttons.
