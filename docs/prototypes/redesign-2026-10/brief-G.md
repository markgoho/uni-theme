# Option G — “Atlas” (key `G`, file `option-g.html`) — BOUNDARY PUSHER

**Thesis:** a spatial reference instrument. The content is a map of knowledge you navigate, not a stack of pages: a radial atlas of badges, a requirement “wheel”, a persistent command palette (⌘K), popovers anchored to the thing they describe. Native platform features only: Popover API, CSS anchor positioning (with fallback), `<dialog>`, view transitions, container queries, `:has()`, variable fonts.

**Look:** instrument-panel calm: cool-neutral graphite and bone surfaces with a **deep Scouting green** primary and **gold** secondary (brass instrument accents); hairline rules on a visible 4px grid; concentric rings and ticks drawn with inline SVG. Dark theme = black-green instrument glass with luminous gold ticks. Type: a geometric sans with wide/compressed range (e.g. **Outfit**, **Sora** or **Manrope**) + a readable body sans (e.g. **Atkinson Hyperlegible** or **Source Sans 3**); tabular numerals everywhere numbers align.

**Required techniques (with fallbacks):** `popover` attribute + `popovertarget` for requirement detail/resources; CSS anchor positioning (`anchor-name`/`position-anchor`/`position-area`) inside `@supports(anchor-name:--a)` with an absolutely-positioned fallback; a global ⌘K / `/` **command palette** (`<dialog>`) on every page that lists awards and requirements from `DATA` and filters as you type (real client-side filter of the loaded data); inline SVG for the radial maps; `view-transition-name` on award identity across pages.

**Structural axes**
- Nav model: **command-palette-first**: the header is a thin instrument bar with the site name, a “⌘K Search” button and the theme toggle; on award/requirements/guide pages a **sticky top “dial” strip** (award identity + section segments + current position) replaces any sidebar. Mobile: same strip, horizontally scrollable.
- Modes: a **ring segment**: children of a `select` mode are drawn as a small ring with N segments and “choose 1” in the centre; `all` mode as a filled ring; the same glyph sits in the stem row.
- Requirement list form: **split instrument**: left (or top on mobile) a compact tracklist of top-level requirements with rings; right a detail pane showing the selected requirement’s full verbatim text, children and resources — ALL requirements must also be present in the DOM and readable without JS (render all detail panes, show the selected one via `:target`/`:has(:checked)` or JS) so the page is complete at rest. Guide links as popovers anchored to the requirement row.
- Guide chrome: **split view**: the requirement text pinned in a left instrument card (sticky) while the guide prose scrolls on the right; images open in a `<dialog>` lightbox; callouts as instrument panels with a small gauge-like icon; guide nav as a radial “chapter dial” (SVG) at the top that is also a real link list.
- Search grouping: the search PAGE is the command palette expanded: facets as chips, results as a **two-pane finder** (awards on the left with hit counts, selected award’s hits on the right); keyboard look-and-feel.

**Home:** MBU — the **atlas**: an inline-SVG radial map of the 14 categories as spokes with badge-count-proportional arcs; the 17 Eagle-required badges orbit as emblem nodes; hovering/focusing a spoke shows a popover with the category; below, a compact grid. SU — a **rank dial**: the 7 ranks as a 7-segment ring with Scout at the start and Eagle at the summit, each segment a link with level, requirements count; the two phases as inner/outer rings; rank cards below.

**Award page:** a **requirement wheel** (inline SVG): N spokes for the top-level requirements, each labelled with its number and curated title, hovering a spoke opens a popover with the verbatim text; facts as instrument readouts; resources as a toolbar.
