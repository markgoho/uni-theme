# Option A — “Handbook” (key `A`, file `option-a.html`)

**Thesis:** the site reads like the printed Scout handbook: a book with a table of contents, chapter openers, hanging numbers, marginalia and plates. Calm, typographic, authoritative.

**Look:** Scouting tan paper and olive-black ink, **handbook red** as the single accent (stamps, rules, requirement numbers), gold only for Eagle marks. Dark = lamp-lit canvas (dark olive-brown ground, paper text). Type: Fraunces (display) + Source Serif 4 (body, verbatim) + Public Sans small caps for labels.

**Structural axes**
- Nav: running-head header; a sticky left **margin column** with chapter contents on inner pages (bottom sheet on mobile).
- Modes: **marginalia** labelled rules (“Do all” / “Do one of the following”) across the block.
- Requirements: **numbered document** with hanging numbers and letters, rules between; curated title as small-caps eyebrow, verbatim text in serif at full size; “See also” references.
- Guide: **book page**: boxed requirement epigraph, drop cap, callouts as margin notes at wide widths, plates with captions.
- Search: **concordance**: headword, entries grouped by award, path in red, no cards.
